## OWN-WORLD

Preserve the warm framed-art exhibition and cool photographic-print language in DESIGN.md. Photography keeps a paper print rather than acquiring an artwork frame. Keep original image proportions, actual titles/dates/events, readable labels, restrained depth, light/dark support, and reduced-motion behavior. Primary actions use Traditional Chinese; Japanese stays secondary.

Keep the author media, social links, creative-history disclosure, and journey/dialogue anchors. The primary navigation reads 當期展, 館藏, and 手記.

## VERIFICATION
